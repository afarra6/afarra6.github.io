# Program Notes

"At the same time, something else entirely is going on: not imitation at all but a capture of code, surplus value of code, an increase in valence, a veritable becoming, a becoming-wasp of the orchid and a becoming-orchid of the wasp" - Gilles Deleuze, A Thousand Plateaus
In occult practices, left hand path magic is often associated with the breaking of taboos and evilness, as well as the rejection of external dieties in a pursuit of self-deification. Left hand path magic, and magic in general, can represent a reassertion of agency over conditions one has no control over. Through the performance of these magics practitioners undergo a process of change, to turn a Deleuzian phrase, becoming-magic. Left Hand Paths explores these concepts of agency, becoming, and otherness over its 8 movements incorporating or inspired by occult texts including Helena Blavatsky's "Secret Doctrines", Eliphas Levi's "Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie", the Chaldean Oracles, and the Mithras Liturgy.
